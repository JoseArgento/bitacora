# Changelog

## 0.2.0 (unreleased)

- **From the screenshots of 5/11, 12:04**: the **reminders form** is part of the book now: a wide field for what to remember, the kind as a row of chips (a zone, a place, day and time, before the reset, a level, logging in), only the field that kind needs with a short help under it, the checks on one row and the button under them on the right; places are chips too; the long explanation moved into a "How does it work?" tooltip. In the list each reminder wears a tag of its kind, and its menu can **edit** it (the form loads it and saves in place). Progress: class colours, times per level and dungeon counts are darkened for the parchment (the white priest and yellow rogue names could not be read), old "Unknown" companions are dropped, the "no dungeons" line no longer gets cut, and the achievements say where they come from (the game's Legacy challenges, by category) and open them on click. The notebook's images row has air above it and its label and button line up; the album's buttons no longer sit on its footnote. Pergamino 4 (text areas stay in ink).
- **From the second round of screenshots** (5/11): a **minimap button** of its own (the book; a bell for the reminders addons; drag it around the minimap, right click for options, can be hidden in Options; each part keeps its own place); a small **Options bookmark** under the others; Progress lists dungeons even when the Adventure Guide was never opened (it walks the journal's tiers, and otherwise asks you to open it once) and no longer stopped with an error there (`EJ_SelectTier(0)`); "1 group"; group members whose name had not arrived ("Unknown") are not written down; the welcome card grows to its text with the button under it; on Recordame's single page the "only this character" check is no longer hidden behind the button; the parts turned off by the full addon say so in one line, once; "Levels 10 to 12 · Elwynn Forest" without the stray period; the album and the notebook say that `BitacoraImagenes` has to be created by hand (an addon cannot create folders), now step 1 of the install; `Bindings.xml` is no longer listed in the TOC (the game loads it by name; listed, it raised XML warnings); Pergamino 2 (wider bookmarks in one line, scroll bars only when needed).
- **Progress**: time per level, with the account's fastest character for each level (the count follows the addon's own sessions and re-anchors to `/played`); **companions**, who you grouped with (class, groups shared, last seen where), account-wide and capped at 500.
- **Reminders**: snooze 5/10/15 min from a small popup (optional); day-and-time reminders that came due while you were logged out fire at login, once, looking back up to 30 days; a setting to follow the realm's clock instead of your computer's. Same in Recordame.
- Web: time per level and companions in the journal overview, both looks.
- **Forever in the journal**: Legacy challenges with their points (and the character's and account's totals in Progress), the first campsite, blueprints learned. "Where was I" at login after a few days away.
- **Reminders from the game's calendar**: guild events of the next 7 days are offered once each (Yes / No / Always), ringing 60 minutes before in local time. **Whispered reminders** (`/bita a Name: text`, `/recordame a …`), accepted only from guildmates and friends. A warning 5 minutes before the campsite buff ends.
- Web: **characters side by side** and **the guild chronicle** week by week with a postcard of the week. The chronicle now keeps achievement and item names so the page can word them.
- **The window is now an open book** (the shared Pergamino look, vendored in `Bitacora_Datos/Libs/Pergamino`): two parchment pages in a leather cover, cloth bookmarks as tabs, a title banner, a wax seal, ink typography with the addon's own OFL fonts (Cinzel, Alegreya), and no Blizzard frame, tab, button or inset templates. Every tab now uses both pages (list on the left, detail on the right); the reminders-only addons use a single page. The old "Logbook / Tavern" setting is gone: the book is the only look.
- **In the client**: the start entry no longer reads "in ." when the zone text was still empty (it fills in once the zone arrives); the milestones view folds consecutive level-ups of a day into "Levels 2 to 9"; chat lines are measured (`settings.chat`: never / important once per session / all; `/bita chat …`; the old "screenshot chat line" option became "never"); the addon's **language can be chosen** (`/bita idioma es|en|auto`, also in Options) so Spanish works on an English client; a **welcome card** the first time the book opens and friendlier empty pages; **bookmark tooltips** with the page and the key binding; own icons in the addon compartment (book; a bell for the reminders addons). The options page is drawn on first use, in the chosen language.
- Look: the book cover is drawn for the window's shape (the compass rose no longer stretches); list rows take ink and gold on the tavern skin instead of the default teal; on the tavern web page the open ribbon and the main button are gold and crimson rather than blue, a pushpin that floated beside the notes is gone, and on a phone the tabs fit and the level chart keeps readable labels.

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

First version, for WoW Forever 1.60.1.

- **Notebook**: tabs, search, item/quest/achievement links, notes attached to NPCs, items and zones (shown in tooltips and on arrival), up to 4 images per note.
- **Journal**: writes itself (start, levels, first visit of each zone, bosses, deaths, quests, loot of a chosen quality, achievements, gold, time played) plus your own entries, milestones filter and search.
- **Album**: every screenshot noted with when, where, level and group; captions.
- **Reminders**: by zone, level, login, mailbox, bank, vendor, auction house, day and time, or hours before the weekly reset; per character or for all, once or repeating. Officers can send day-and-time reminders to the whole guild.
- **Progress**: your numbers, every dungeon and raid of the Adventure Guide with the bosses killed, achievements by category.
- **Guild chronicle** (off by default): milestones shared with guildmates who have the addon.
- **Tavern look**: parchment pages, wooden title bar and a book cover that opens.
- Standalone parts (Libreta, Diario, Album, Recordatorios) sharing `Bitacora_Datos`, and **Recordame** as a fully independent reminders addon.
- Web companion (two looks) that reads the save file: journal day by day with search and a level chart, album paired with your screenshots, notebook, postcards of a day, Markdown export, image tool.
- Handles Forever beta quirks: double `PLAYER_DEAD`, stale zone text after travel, double window events.
